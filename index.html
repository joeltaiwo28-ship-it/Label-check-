What's in this?

Snap the ingredients on the back of a pack, or type them. You'll see every ingredient, and which ones are linked to cancer risk.

Guidance only, not medical advice. "Linked to" means research found an association; risk depends on how much and how often. Ingredients we don't have data on show as unknown, which does not mean safe. Based on IARC classifications and EFSA/EU decisions. Starter list of about 30 items, not yet reviewed by a doctor or toxicologist.
